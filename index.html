Spearo's Dive Report

Wind, swell, tide, weather and water temp, cross-checked across multiple models and NOAA, then graded A to F. Nothing is guessed: missing data shows as “no data”, and no grade is given without wind, gust and wave data.

Forecast data covers about 16 days ahead; past dates use model hindcasts, and tide/water-temp observations come from NOAA (US stations only). Times are local to the site.
Good hunting, stay safe. Never dive alone, always fly a dive flag, and check local spearfishing rules and regulations. This report is a planning aid, not a safety clearance.
