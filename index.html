Spearo's Dive Report

Grades wind, gusts, waves, swell, weather, tide, water temperature and current for a dive site, cross-checked across several models and NOAA.

Data sources:

  • Open-Meteo: forecast, marine and geocoding
  • NOAA CO-OPS: tides and water temperature (US)
  • National Weather Service: alerts (US)
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: place lookup

This is a planning aid, not a safety clearance. Never dive alone, always fly a dive flag, and check local rules.
